Data & methodology

Monthly, not seasonally adjusted Producer Price Index data from the US Bureau of Labor Statistics, retrieved through FRED. All series use 1982 = 100. An index is not a dollar price or a supplier quote.

Monthly change = 100 × (index / index one calendar month earlier − 1). Yearly change uses the same calendar month one year earlier. Volatility is the sample standard deviation of 12 consecutive monthly percentage changes, in percentage points; it is not annualized.

Calculations use the full history before filtering dates, preserving the lookback at the beginning of your selection. Missing observations remain gaps; there is no interpolation. Invalid dates and nonpositive or nonnumeric values are excluded; duplicate months use the last valid observation. Partial volatility windows are not calculated.

A line chart shows index trends, diverging columns show monthly direction, a zero-centered line shows yearly inflation, and an area chart shows changing volatility. Chart zoom is independent; the date toolbar applies to every chart in the active date-range view. Each material has its own views; dates, chart choices and zoom are kept when switching views. Closing the last view creates a fresh default. View settings are saved in this browser; Browser storage availability varies when opening a local file.

The included data snapshot works entirely offline. To update a material, download its CSV from FRED while online and use Import FRED CSV. Imported data stays in this browser when storage is available. Save portable HTML creates a copy with the currently loaded data for sharing.
